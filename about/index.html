Who We Are

Institutional Overview

The Center for Disaster Management and Sustainable Development (CDMSD) is a registered independent multi-disciplinary consulting firm based in Pakistan.

CDMSD was established with cross-sector expertise and experience in disaster risk management, sustainable development, disaster risk reduction (DRR), climate change adaptation, WASH, governance, and community development.

Public institutional documentation identifies CDMSD as “the first ever consulting initiative established to provide specialized services in disaster risk management in Pakistan.”

Our Mission

“To provide cutting edge solutions to our clients based on best practices in disaster management and sustainable Development.”

— Quoted Mission Statement (Public Organizational Record)
Our Approach

CDMSD combines technical knowledge, field experience and multidisciplinary approaches to address disaster risk, climate vulnerability and sustainable development challenges.

Our work integrates rigorous baseline assessments, international evaluation criteria (OECD-DAC / Sphere Standards), and participatory community mobilization to ensure sustainable, dignified outcomes for vulnerable populations.

Scope of Work

CDMSD’s documented scope ranges from grassroots-level community work to national-level medium- and short-term assignments.

Institutional Independence Note

Center for Disaster Management and Sustainable Development (CDMSD) is an independent consulting firm founded in 2013. It is distinct and separate from the academic Center for Disaster Management (CDM) at the University of Management and Technology (UMT), Lahore.

2013 Public Record Verified

CDMSD Established in Pakistan

Founded as a registered independent multi-disciplinary consulting firm with the mandate to provide specialized services in disaster risk management and sustainable development.

2014–2015 Documented Field Touchpoint

Post-Flood Loss Assessment & WASH Recovery

Documented professional engagement assessing losses, rural WASH access, and disaster risk reduction future planning across flood-affected communities in Jhang, Muzaffargarh (Punjab), and Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK).

Documented Assignment ALNAP / DEC Cited

Thar Desert Multi-Sector Emergency Response Evaluation

Commissioned as the national consultancy firm to conduct the independent evaluation of the Pakistan Multi-sector Integrated Emergency Response in drought-affected Thar Desert, Sindh.

2019 (Jan–Feb) SBDRM Certification

Master Trainer Certification — SBDRM Islamabad

Facilitated intensive four-day School-Based Disaster Risk Management training in Islamabad, certifying 35 higher-secondary school educators as Master Trainers.

Documented Study SDC Resilience Study

Bajaur & Mohmand Livelihood and DRR Study

Documented evaluation titled “Livelihood and DRR Initiatives for Community Uplift in Bajaur and Mohmand Agencies” cited in development-sector resilience research.
