Climate Change Adaptation

Climate vulnerability assessments, localized adaptation roadmaps, and drought resilience strategies for Pakistan's vulnerable ecosystems.

Overview

Evidence-Based Climate Adaptation

Pakistan ranks among the most climate-vulnerable countries globally. CDMSD assists development partners and public bodies in diagnosing localized climate risks and implementing practical adaptation mechanisms.

Our multidisciplinary team evaluates climate stresses on water availability, rural agriculture, livestock health, and socio-economic systems in arid, riverine, and mountainous terrains.

Key Consulting Capabilities

  • Climate Vulnerability and Risk Assessments (CVRA) at district and union council levels
  • Community-Based Adaptation (CBA) planning and participatory action plans
  • Drought preparedness, water harvesting, and groundwater recharge strategies
  • Extreme temperature and heatwave coping evaluations
  • Integration of climate resilience metrics into public development programs
