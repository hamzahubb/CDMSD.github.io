Community Development

Participatory rural appraisal, grassroots institutional strengthening, and gender-inclusive mobilization for durable community resilience.

Overview

Grassroots Mobilization & Institution Building

Sustainable resilience begins with empowered communities. CDMSD facilitates inclusive community mobilization that strengthens local decision-making bodies and fosters collective action against disaster vulnerabilities.

We prioritize participatory methodologies that engage women, youth, elders, and vulnerable minority groups, ensuring interventions reflect genuine local priorities and build long-term local ownership.

Core Capabilities

  • Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) and Community Action Planning (CAP)
  • Capacity building for Community Organizations (COs) and Village Organizations (VOs)
  • Gender inclusion and protection of vulnerable groups in crisis-affected areas
  • Grassroots conflict sensitivity and social cohesion assessments
  • Local community governance and maintenance committee training
