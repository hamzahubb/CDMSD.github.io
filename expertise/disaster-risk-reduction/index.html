Disaster Risk Reduction & DRM

Multi-hazard risk assessment, community preparedness frameworks, and school safety capacity building across Pakistan.

Overview & Mandate

Specialized Disaster Risk Management Solutions

As the first consulting initiative established to provide specialized services in disaster risk management in Pakistan, CDMSD provides technical consulting across the entire disaster risk lifecycle.

Our capabilities span multi-hazard risk assessment, local contingency planning, early warning dissemination mechanisms, and community-based disaster risk management (CBDRM/SBDRM).

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-hazard disaster risk assessment & mapping (floods, droughts, earthquakes)
  • School-Based Disaster Risk Management (SBDRM) & Master Trainer Certification
  • Community-Based Disaster Risk Management (CBDRM) & Village Disaster Management Committees
  • Disaster loss assessments, rapid damage profiling, and recovery frameworks
  • Emergency contingency planning & institutional preparedness audits

Field-Tested Methodology

CDMSD designs all DRM interventions in alignment with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction (2015–2030), National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) guidelines, and Sphere Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Response.
