Food Security & Livelihoods

Livelihood recovery assessments, rural economic diversification, and drought-coping food security evaluation across Pakistan.

Overview

Restoring Economic Dignity & Food Security

Disasters disrupt household income, decimate livestock, and destroy agricultural crops. CDMSD delivers rigorous Emergency Food Security and Livelihood (EFSL) evaluations and recovery planning for rural farming and pastoralist communities.

Applying the Sustainable Livelihoods Framework, we analyze human, natural, financial, social, and physical capital assets to identify resilient economic strategies and evaluate the impact of cash-for-work, vocational training, and asset replacement programs.

Core Capabilities

  • Emergency Food Security and Livelihood Assessments (EFSL)
  • Drought-resilient agricultural and livestock support evaluation
  • Cash transfers, voucher programs, and asset recovery assessments
  • Household economic vulnerability profiling & coping mechanism analysis
  • Micro-enterprise and vocational livelihood impact studies
