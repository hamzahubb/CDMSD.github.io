Overview

Strengthening Public Systems & Institutional Capacity

Effective disaster management and sustainable development require robust institutional mandates, transparent governance, and coordinated operational frameworks.

CDMSD advises government departments, disaster management authorities, and civil society alliances in streamlining policy guidelines, strengthening district-level coordination, and institutionalizing disaster preparedness standards.

Core Capabilities

  • Institutional capacity and readiness diagnostics for DRM authorities
  • Policy review, regulatory compliance, and roadmap formulation
  • Inter-agency coordination mechanisms between public departments and humanitarian actors
  • Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) frameworks and feedback loops
  • Stakeholder consultation workshops and consensus-building processes
