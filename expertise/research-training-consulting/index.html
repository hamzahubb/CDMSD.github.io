Overview

Independent Third-Party Evaluations & Capacity Development

CDMSD has established a proven track record delivering independent project evaluations and specialized capacity-building programs across Pakistan.

Our research adheres strictly to international evaluation principles (OECD-DAC criteria: Relevance, Coherence, Effectiveness, Efficiency, Impact, and Sustainability), employing mixed-methods quantitative surveys, key informant interviews, and participatory focus groups.

Core Capabilities

  • Independent project evaluations (Baseline, Mid-Term, and Endline studies)
  • Training of Trainers (ToT) and School-Based DRM Master Trainer Certification
  • Mixed-methods research design, quantitative household surveys, and focus group moderation
  • Curriculum development and training manual authoring for disaster risk reduction
  • Policy briefs, case studies, and institutional knowledge products
