WASH (Water, Sanitation & Hygiene)

Clean drinking water access assessments, disaster-resilient water infrastructure reviews, and hygiene promotion in crisis-affected and drought-prone communities.

Overview

Technical WASH Consulting & Field Evaluations

Access to safe drinking water and dignified sanitation is a fundamental determinant of resilience. CDMSD delivers technical needs assessments, third-party monitoring, and impact evaluations for emergency and development WASH schemes across Pakistan.

Our specialists evaluate the structural integrity, water quality, community governance, and maintenance viability of water filtration plants, solar reverse-osmosis systems, hand pumps, and sanitation facilities.

Core Capabilities

  • WASH technical needs assessments during disaster relief and early recovery
  • Clean drinking water infrastructure assessments (solar pumps, filtration, storage)
  • Community water management committee capacity reviews and sustainability models
  • Sanitation, hygiene promotion, and open-defecation-free (ODF) verification studies
  • Disaster-resilient water systems engineering reviews against flood and drought risks
