About the Center

Experience rooted in disaster risk management and sustainable development.

The Center for Disaster Management and Sustainable Development (CDMSD) is a registered independent multi-disciplinary consulting firm established in Pakistan in 2013.

Its work spans grassroots initiatives to national-level assignments, with core capabilities across disaster risk reduction, climate change adaptation, WASH, governance, food security, and community development.

Documented Scope & Positioning

Public institutional records describe CDMSD as “the first ever consulting initiative established to provide specialized services in disaster risk management in Pakistan,” designed to support international development organizations, government departments, civil society, and the corporate sector.

Institutional Methodology

Our Tri-Pillar Approach

CDMSD combines technical knowledge, field experience and multidisciplinary approaches to address disaster risk, climate vulnerability and sustainable development challenges.

01

Understand Risk

Evidence, multi-hazard assessment, and empirical research. We analyze baseline vulnerabilities, meteorological patterns, and community hazard profiles before designing interventions.

02

Strengthen Resilience

Capacity development, community mobilization, and institutional readiness. We train Master Trainers, equip local committees, and build resilient infrastructure systems.

03

Support Sustainable Development

Long-term, practical, and context-sensitive solutions. We ensure that recovery transitions smoothly into sustainable livelihood security and institutionalized governance.
