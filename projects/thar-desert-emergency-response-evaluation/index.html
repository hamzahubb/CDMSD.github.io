Multi-sector Integrated Emergency Response & Drought Resilience Evaluation

Assignment Context

Independent Evaluation in Drought-Affected Thar Desert

In an independent evaluation report for the Disasters Emergency Committee (DEC) / Care International multi-sector relief project, CDMSD is documented as the national consultancy firm conducting the field evaluation in the drought-affected Thar Desert of Sindh.

Verified Evaluation Scope

  • National Consultancy Firm Role: Commissioned to assess project relevance, coverage, efficiency, effectiveness, and sustainability across drought-affected settlements.
  • Multi-Sector Scope: Evaluated clean drinking water schemes, education interventions, child protection, sustainable livelihoods, and emergency early recovery.
  • Geographic Location: Thar Desert region, Sindh, Pakistan.

Methodology & Evaluation Focus

The evaluation deployed mixed-methods fieldwork across remote desert villages, combining household surveys, community focus group discussions, key informant interviews with local stakeholders, and physical technical inspection of water points and livelihood assets against Sphere Standards.
