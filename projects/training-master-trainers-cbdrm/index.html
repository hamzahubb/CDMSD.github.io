Training of Master Trainers — School & Community Based Disaster Risk Management

Assignment Overview

Institutionalizing School Safety & Disaster Preparedness

CDMSD designed and delivered a dedicated School-Based Disaster Risk Management (SBDRM) Master Trainer program in Islamabad to build institutional capacity within the educational system.

Verified Deliverables

  • 4-Day SBDRM Curriculum: Facilitated intensive instructional sessions covering hazard identification, vulnerability assessment, early warning, and school safety audits.
  • 35 Master Trainers Certified: Certified 35 Higher-Secondary School Teachers as institutional Master Trainers to cascade DRM protocols.
  • Conducted in Islamabad: Delivered during January – February 2019.

CDMSD's Documented Role

The consultancy team was responsible for curriculum delivery, pedagogical facilitation, simulation drill supervision, and participant competency evaluation.
